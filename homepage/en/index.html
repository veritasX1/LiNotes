Notes Like Apple’s, on Your Own Server

LiNotes brings the simplicity of Apple’s Notes to Android and Ubuntu and stores your data encrypted on your own server.

Notes

Everything in One Note

From a quick idea to trip planning with a budget.

Formatting

Titles, headings, bullets, numbered lists, quotes and checklists with round checkmarks, each with one tap.

Tables in Notes

Budget, packing list, comparison: insert a table, start writing, Tab to the next cell. At the end of the table the new row appears by itself.

Calculating with =

Type a calculation and an equals sign, and LiNotes writes the result after it. Decimal commas, brackets and operator precedence are respected.

Voice Recordings and PDFs

One tap on the microphone records the conversation into the note, playable on every device. Letters from school, invoices and plans sit right next to it as PDFs.

Collapse Sections

Long notes stay clear: an arrow before the heading collapses the whole section, on the phone as on the computer.

List or Gallery

As a list or a gallery, sorted by date or title, in folders and subfolders. Pinned notes stay at the top.

New in 2.1 to 2.3

More Features

Linking Notes

Type >> and choose a note. The link stays up to date, even if you rename it.

Mentions

Address someone in shared notes with @Name. They get a notification.

What’s New?

If someone has changed a shared note, you see a dot and the changed lines are marked.

Highlighting in Six Colors

Yellow, orange, pink, purple, mint and blue, plus text color, font and alignment.

Justified Text and Hyphenation

Clean typesetting on the phone. Titles and headings are left out.

Locked Notes

Encrypt secrets additionally. Unlock with your fingerprint on the phone.

Quick Note

From the phone’s quick settings or with a keyboard shortcut on the computer, and you start writing right away.

PDF and Printing

Every note and plan as a typeset PDF to share, print or post.

Separate Window

On the computer, a double-click opens a note in its own window, so you can work side by side.

Drag and Drop

Drag notes, lists, boards and plans onto a folder, with the mouse on the computer or a finger on the phone. Folders can be nested as deep as you like.

Keyboard Shortcuts

All shortcuts at a glance with Ctrl + ?.

Camera

Photos go straight into the note the right way up, portrait too.

Text Size

Set per device, for example large on the phone and compact on the laptop.

Tap Links

Web addresses in notes open directly.

There is a template for cleaning rotas, timetables, duty rosters and projects. Today is always highlighted.

Cleaning rota with rotation: the names move on one row every week. Anyone on holiday simply gets an entry of their own.

Rotation

For cleaning and duty rotas: enter the names once, LiNotes redistributes week after week.

Columns as You Like

Free columns, weekdays, single days or calendar weeks.

For the Notice Board

As a landscape PDF, for the fridge, the ward office or the clubhouse.

Tasks

Tasks on a Board

Boards with columns, colors, priorities and due dates. Together with family or team: what one person moves, everyone sees at once.

  • Drag cards from “Open” through “In progress” to “Done”.
  • Priority with one, two or three exclamation marks.
  • Report as PDF or CSV for filing or a spreadsheet.

Development projects

From Change Request to Release

One click turns a board into a development project: card IDs, status history, impact analysis, verification, version and linked commits. The traceability matrix is created at the push of a button.

Example: a fictitious automotive project (lane keeping assist) with an ASPICE-like workflow.

Every Change Request Traceable

  • Impact analysis: what is affected, which safety goal, which ASIL rating.
  • Verification: SIL, HIL and road test evidence as files right on the card, with a checksum in the report.
  • Commits automatically: if the card ID is in the commit message, LiNotes links the two.
  • Status history: when the card moved from analysis to implementation, and by whom.

The Traceability Matrix

Right-click the board and choose “Export Report” to create the record with matrix and a detail page per card, as a PDF for the audit or a CSV for further processing.

Not Even the Operator

Notes, lists, plans, folder names, images and audio are encrypted before they leave your device. The server only sees unreadable data.

Verified People

Before you share, you confirm each other once by code or QR code. That way nobody can slip in a false key.

Devices Like Signal

You add a new device with a six-digit code or a QR code.

Your own server

Raspberry Pi, Debian, Ubuntu

Without a server, choose “Use without server” on first launch. You can add a server any time later.
