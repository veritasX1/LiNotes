LiNotes brings the simplicity of Apple’s Notes to Android and Ubuntu – and keeps your data where it belongs: with you.

Notes

Notes that think along.

From a quick idea to a trip plan with a budget – all in one note.

Write like on paper.
Only neater.

Titles, headings, bulleted lists, numbered lists, quotes and checklists with round ticks. One tap is enough – and everything looks typeset.

Tables. Right in the note.

Budget, packing list, comparison: insert a table, start writing, Tab to the next cell. At the end of the table the new row appears by itself.

Calculating with =

Type a calculation and an equals sign – LiNotes writes the result after it. With decimal commas and brackets, multiplication before addition. No calculator needed.

Record speech.
Attach PDFs.

One tap on the microphone and the conversation lands in the note – playable on every device. Letters from school, invoices and plans sit right next to it as PDFs.

Expand what you need.

Long notes stay clear: an arrow in front of the heading collapses the whole section – the same on phone and computer.

Everything at a glance.

As a list or a gallery, sorted by date or title, in folders and subfolders. Pinned items stay at the top – just as you know it.

Tasks

What’s coming up.
What’s done.

Boards with columns, colours, priorities and due dates. Together with family or team – what one person moves, everyone sees immediately.

  • Drag cards from “Open” through “In progress” to “Done”.
  • Priority with one, two or three exclamation marks.
  • Report as PDF or CSV – for filing or the spreadsheet.

Development projects

Ready for series production.

One click turns a board into a development project: card IDs, status history, impact analysis, verification, version and linked commits – up to a traceability matrix at the push of a button.

Example: a fictitious automotive project (lane keeping assist) with an ASPICE-like workflow.

Every change request.
Fully traceable.

  • Impact analysis – what is affected, which safety goal, which ASIL rating.
  • Verification – SIL, HIL and road test evidence as files right on the card, with checksum in the report.
  • Commits automatically – card ID in the commit message, and LiNotes links them.
  • Status history – when did the card go from analysis to implementation, and who moved it.

The traceability matrix.
In one second.

Right-click the board, “Export report” – and the evidence with matrix and detail pages for every card is ready. As a PDF for the audit or as CSV for further processing.
